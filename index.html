MORE THAN A MEETUP

Built for active locals who view stand-up paddleboarding as a sport, a mental reset, and don't mind the occasional cold weather.

💪

Core & Endurance

Turn your board into a floating gym. Interval training, distance sessions, and posture refinement designed for a full-body workout.

❄️

Cold-Water Skill

Fall, winter, and spring paddling are magic in Central Oregon. We teach cold-water safety, thermal layering, and river mechanics.

🤝

Newbie Friendly

No gatekeeping. We host dedicated onboarding clinics so brand-new paddlers can build skill and confidence before main workouts.
